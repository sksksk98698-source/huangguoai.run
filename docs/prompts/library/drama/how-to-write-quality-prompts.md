## 二、消除AI感的四大核心物理学心法

### 心法一：用“生理肌肉联动”取代“死板表情词”

- **错误示范**：`angry, furious face, screaming`（容易生成面部扭曲融化的怪脸）
- **大师心法**：不直接说愤怒，而是描写愤怒引起的生理反应。
- **高级写法**：`Subtle muscle twitch along the jawline, flared nostrils, rapid shallow breathing causing chest rise and fall, piercing predator stare with slightly narrowed eyelids, high-definition skin pores.`

### 心法二：用“真实次表面散射与微瑕”取代“磨皮假面”

- **错误示范**：`smooth skin, perfect face, porcelain flawless skin`（极度塑料、像假人）
- **大师心法**：真实的皮肤在逆光下有血液透光的次表面散射（Subsurface Scattering），且存在自然纹理。
- **高级写法**：`Subsurface scattering of earlobes under backlighting, micro skin textures, natural freckles, fine peach fuzz on cheeks, authentic sebaceous glint, unretouched raw 35mm photography.`

### 心法三：用“虚拟摄影机物理光学”取代“泛称电影感”

- **错误示范**：`cinematic lighting, movie style, epic shot`
- **大师心法**：指定具体的摄影器材型号、焦段与光圈，迫使扩散模型调用电影数据集中的特定权重。
- **高级写法**：`Shot on ARRI Alexa LF, Cooke Anamorphic/i 50mm Prime lens, shallow depth of field with creamy oval bokeh, subtle horizontal anamorphic blue streak lens flare, Kodak Vision3 250D film stock grain.`

### 心法四：用“环境力学交互”取代“静态悬浮人像”

- **错误示范**：`standing in the rain`
- **大师心法**：雨滴打在衣服上会有吸收变深色，打在地面有水花溅起，微风吹拂发丝有动力学飘动。
- **高级写法**：`Torrential rain soaking into heavy wool overcoat causing visible fabric darkening, water droplets trickling down the rim of the collar, atmospheric rain mist softening the distant neon street lamps, micro water splashes around leather boots on wet asphalt.`
